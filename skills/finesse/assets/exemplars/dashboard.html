Other files: Controls Surfaces Data Marketing

  1. Four KPI tiles, four visual types: a line, a segmented meter, diverging bars, a status strip.
  2. Every figure has a unit, a comparison and a visual. None stands alone.
  3. Series colours come from --chart-1 to --chart-6. The lead hue is the accent hue.
  4. Colour never works alone: the forecast is dashed, legend markers change shape, status badges change glyph, bad days are shorter bars.
  5. The dotted grid sits on the tick values. Axis labels are HTML at 12px, so they never scale with the drawing.
  6. The plot is one focusable slider. Arrow keys move an hour, Page keys a day, and a hidden table gives daily totals.
  7. The donut is drawn from the table next to it. The table is the text alternative.
  8. Tiles sit one step above the page: a 1px ring, a top highlight, a short gradient.
  9. One tinted tile per screen, and it shows its evidence.
  10. Loading keeps every tile the same size. Empty shows a ghost of the chart and one primary action. Try ?state=loading and ?state=empty.
  11. At 1024 the sidebar is an icon rail. At 390 it is a tab bar and the KPI tiles sit two by two.
