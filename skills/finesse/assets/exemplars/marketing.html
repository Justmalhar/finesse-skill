01

Three heroes

None is a centred headline over two buttons. Each makes one decision and commits to it.

  1. Split: copy on five columns, product on seven. The product fragment is real UI, cropped by the frame, never a floating screenshot.
  2. The fragment tells the headline’s story: two curves, the gap between them shaded, one callout.
  3. Type-led: the headline sits on the time axis of a roast and the curve runs behind it. No image is needed.
  4. Editorial: six ruled columns run the full height. Every block starts on a rule.
  5. Real quotation marks hang outside the text edge. The drop cap takes the accent, once.
  6. One primary button per hero. The second action is a text link.

02

Feature section

Three claims, three kinds of evidence. Not three icon cards.

  1. Each row proves its claim with a different specimen: a figure, a number, a quotation.
  2. The lead column holds one heading and one link, and stays put.
  3. Rows are separated by hairlines, numbered in mono, with no boxes.
  4. The pull quote is set in the display face with its opening mark hung.

Everything a roast leaves behind, kept in one place.

Batchbook is a log first. It does not tell you how to roast. It makes sure you can find out what you did.

03

Pricing and footer

One surface with three columns. The plan most people need is raised, not badged.

  1. The currency sign and the period are small and quiet. The figure is tabular and set in the display face.
  2. Switching the billing period swaps the price in place with a blur. Nothing changes width.
  3. Later plans list only what they add.
  4. The raised plan overlaps the well by 12 px, top and bottom, and takes the one primary button.
  5. The footer wordmark is cropped by the frame at under 8% contrast: identity as texture.
  6. The status line is a real link with a state, not decoration.
