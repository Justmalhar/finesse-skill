01

Table

Dense, calm, and readable down a column as well as across a row.

  1. Numbers are right-aligned and tabular. Decimals are quieter than the integer, and the unit lives in the header.
  2. Loss carries a bar against the 15% target, so a column of figures can be scanned for outliers. Over target turns amber.
  3. Status is a glyph and words. Resting shows its seven days as ticks.
  4. The header is translucent and gains a shadow only after the body scrolls.
  5. Sort arrows show both directions and light the active one.
  6. Selecting rows raises an inverse bar over the table. Nothing in the layout moves.

02

Empty, loading, error

The three states a table spends real time in. Each keeps the frame of the loaded view.

  1. Empty says what will appear, how to get the first row, and offers one primary action.
  2. Its drawing is a ghost of the real chart, not a stock illustration.
  3. Skeleton bars match the row: a square where the checkbox is, short bars where numbers are.
  4. One sweep crosses the whole block. Under reduced motion it stops.
  5. The error names the cause in plain words and puts the retry next to it.

03

Chart

Drawn in SVG, labelled in HTML, so lines scale and text does not.

  1. Three gridlines, not ten. The unit appears once, on the top label.
  2. Events are labelled on the chart where they happen, not in a legend.
  3. The development phase is a tinted band, because the span is the story.
  4. The tooltip is pinned to a point by default and follows pointer or arrow keys.
  5. Strokes keep their weight at any width through non-scaling-stroke.
