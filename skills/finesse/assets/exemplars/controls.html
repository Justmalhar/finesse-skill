01

Buttons

One primary per view. Depth comes from light, not from a bigger shadow.

  1. Filled buttons carry a 1 px top highlight and a ring one step darker than the fill.
  2. The icon side has 12 px of padding, the text side 16 px.
  3. Press scales to 0.97 and swaps the drop shadow for an inner one.
  4. Idle, loading, success and error faces share one grid cell. The width never moves.
  5. Disabled keeps the shape and loses the depth.

02

Text input and select

A field is a shallow well: it sits below the surface the buttons sit above.

  1. Inner top shadow plus a 1 px light lip under the bottom edge.
  2. The focus ring wraps the frame, affix included, never the bare input.
  3. Help and error share one reserved line, so the form does not jump.
  4. Units are small caps on a tinted affix. Numbers are tabular.
  5. Error adds an icon and words. Colour is never the only signal.

03

Checkbox, switch, segmented

Native inputs underneath. The drawing changes, the behaviour does not.

  1. The tick wipes in with a clip-path, left to right, the way a pen draws it.
  2. The switch thumb stretches toward its destination while held.
  3. Segmented radii are concentric: track 10, padding 4, thumb 6.
  4. In dark themes the thumb on a light accent turns dark, so it stays visible.
  5. Selected text changes colour, never weight.

04

Tabs

The indicator travels. Arrow keys move between tabs, Home and End jump.

  1. One bar slides with transform. It is never four bars fading.
  2. Counts sit in a tabular chip so the labels do not shift as numbers change.
  3. The panel keeps a fixed minimum height across tabs.
  4. Decimals and units are quieter than the figure they belong to.
