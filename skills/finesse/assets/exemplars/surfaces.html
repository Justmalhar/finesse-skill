01

Cards

Five ways to hold content. None of them is a hairline box with a soft glow.

  1. Raised: a ring, then shadows that double in size and never pass 12 px of blur.
  2. The stat card has no chrome. The figure does the work, and its decimal and unit step back.
  3. The image sits in a 4 px frame, so its radius is 16 minus 4. Its hairline is drawn inside it.
  4. A well is lit from above: shadow inside the top edge, highlight under the bottom edge.
  5. Row hover is a tint inset from the card edge. Separators start where the text starts.
  6. Status is a glyph that means something, then plain words. No pills.

02

Overlays

Each one is drawn open on a canvas. The button in the corner opens the live version.

  1. Dialog and sheet share one footer: a tinted strip with the actions, flush to the edge.
  2. The sheet floats 8 px inside the viewport and keeps four corners. On a phone it rises from the bottom with a handle.
  3. Menu radii are concentric: 12 outside, 4 of padding, 8 on the items. Separators run edge to edge.
  4. Stacked toasts step back 8 px and 0.05 of scale each, and hide their text.
  5. The tooltip inverts the page colours, so it needs no border in either theme.
  6. The dotted ground fades out before the edge. Texture marks the canvas and stops there.
