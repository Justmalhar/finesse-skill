What Quill does before an agent opens the ticket.

It drafts from two things you already have: the articles your customers can read, and the replies your team has already written.

Where the draft appears

  1. It reads your help centre

    Quill indexes every published article in Zendesk Guide or Intercom Articles and rereads them when they change. It drafts only from what is published, so a draft is never more generous than your policy.

  2. It learns how your team writes

    From the last 5,000 replies your agents sent, Quill picks up the greeting, the sign-off, how long a reply runs and the phrases your team never uses.

  3. An agent edits it and sends it

    The draft waits in the ticket sidebar. Agents change it as they would their own writing, and Quill keeps their edits, so next week’s drafts need fewer of them.
