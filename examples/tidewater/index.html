A whole week on one page, and a sentence for every day.

Every spot gets the same seven rows. Read across a row for one day; read down a column to see the swell build or the wind turn.

  1. 1

    Tide. The day’s curve from midnight to midnight, with high-water times and heights. The red band is the best window.

  2. 2

    Swell. Height in metres, period in seconds, and the direction it comes from. Longer period means more power.

  3. 3

    Wind. Mean and gust in knots, the arrow showing where it blows. For breaks, it says offshore, onshore or cross-shore.

  4. 4

    Best window. The hours worth going for, and why, in words you could read out in the car park.
