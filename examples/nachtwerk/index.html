A power station with the power off

02 of 05Built in 1912 for the Lindenau tram depot, shut down in 1991, rented by the night since 2023.

The Turbinenhalle keeps its crane rail, its tiles and one of its three turbines. The sound system stands where unit 3 was. Upstairs, the Schaltwarte still has the 1950s switch desks; the booth is built over them.

Door policy and house rules

03 of 05Read them before you queue. The people on the door have.

The door is not about clothes. It is about whether you came for the night and for the people in it.

You need to be 18 and to carry photo ID. We check every ID, every time, including regulars.

We say no sometimes, and we don’t explain it in the queue. Re-entry with a stamp until 04:00; after that, out is out.

Turned away and think we got it wrong? Write to door@nachtwerk-leipzig.de. A person answers within a week.

  1. Phones stay in pockets on the floor

    We put a sticker over your camera at the door. It stays on until you leave. The Kesselhaus bar is the place for messages.

  2. Ask first, every time

    Touch, a photo, a drink, a dance. A no is a full answer.

  3. The awareness team wears red armbands

    They sit at the Kesselhaus bar all night. Or ask any bar “Is Luisa here?” and someone takes you somewhere quiet.

  4. No racism, sexism, homophobia or transphobia

    Not on the floor, not in the queue, not in the yard. You leave, and the ticket is not refunded.

  5. Water is free at every bar

    Bring the cup back and fill it again. Earplugs are free at the cloakroom.

  6. Leave the yard quietly

    Forty families live on Karl-Heine-Straße. They are the reason we can stay open until ten.
