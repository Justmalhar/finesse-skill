04

A former ship chandler’s on Constitution Street

Isla Moffat and Ruth Okafor opened Petal & Stem in 2019, in a narrow shop that sold rope and lamp oil to Leith’s ships for most of the last century. The hooks are still in the ceiling; we hang eucalyptus from them now.

Est. 201914 Constitution Street, Leith, EH6 7BT

We buy from Scottish growers first. From May to October that means sweet peas from the Borders, dahlias from East Lothian and foliage from a walled garden in Fife. In winter, when Scotland grows almost nothing, we buy from small farms in Cornwall and Lincolnshire, and from the Dutch auction only for what nobody here can grow.

No floral foamSince 2020. Arrangements sit in moss, chicken wire or water.

Every bouquet is made the morning it goes out, wrapped in kraft paper and tissue, and carried across town on one of our two cargo bikes. Nothing sits in a cold store overnight.
