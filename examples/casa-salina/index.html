Casa Salina, salt store since 1880

Eleven rooms in a salt warehouse on the Trapani coast

The house was built in 1880 to store the harvest from the pans outside its doors. The walls are a metre of tufa, the floors are the original terracotta, and the sea is 400 metres west.

II. La cucina

One table, one sitting, what the boats brought in

Dinner is at eight in the old weighing room, twenty-two seats at one long table. Giusi cooks what the Trapani boats landed that morning and bakes the fish in salt from the pans you can see from your chair.

III. Le saline

The salt flats change colour with the season

The pans of the Trapani and Paceco reserve start at the garden wall. Sea water is let in each spring, moves pan to pan as it thickens, and turns pink before the salt is raked out by hand in summer.
