From a step to a sound, in five stages

Every stage of the M8 is analogue, and every stage has its own knobs. Scroll, or pick a stage, to watch the signal change on the scope.

02

28 knobs, 8 keys, one knob per job

No menus and no shift layer. The panel is laid out in the order the signal flows: sound sources top left, sequencer along the front edge, patch bay on the right.
