No. 312Marginalia20 Nov 2026

The awning, the storm, and a very good novel about salt

Dear reader, Tuesday’s wind took the awning clean off its brackets, and for an hour the front table was the wettest place in Norwich. We lost nothing but our dignity and one road atlas.

Which is a fair way into this week’s pick. The Salt Year is about weather, mostly, and about what people do when it ruins their plans. Ruth finished it on the bus and missed her stop by four villages.

Ruth, Dev, Odile, Sam and Kit
