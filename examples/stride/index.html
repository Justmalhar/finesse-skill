Two foams, one plate, and the same 8 mm drop

Tempo 2 was one block of EVA. Tempo 3 splits the midsole: a soft, springy layer under your foot, a firmer carrier under that, and a nylon plate between them that stops the soft layer from collapsing at 4:00 per km.

More bounce per stride

The PEBA layer gives back 81% of the energy you put in at heel strike. Tempo 2’s EVA gave back 72%4.

Stiff where you toe off

The plate rolls you forward from the midfoot. Nylon flexes more than carbon, so the shoe stays kind on an 18 km Sunday run5.
