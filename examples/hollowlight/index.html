Someone keeps putting out the light at Saltmarrow Rock.

Hollowlight is a puzzle-exploration game about the last keeper of a North Sea lighthouse. Read the log, turn the lens, learn the tide, and find out who climbs the tower at 2:14 every night.

I

The last keeper left a logbook, a cat, and a lamp that will not stay lit

October 1911. The Northern Lighthouse Board sends you to Saltmarrow Rock, eleven miles off the Aberdeenshire coast, to replace a keeper named Elias Brand. Brand left no note.

Every night you light the lamp at dusk. Every night, at fourteen minutes past two, it goes out. The tower has seven rooms, one stair and no other way up. As far as the log can tell you, you are alone on the rock.

Hollowlight is about the forty nights that follow: what Brand wrote, what he crossed out, and what the light shows when you point it somewhere it was never meant to go.

No combat and no timers. You can stand in the lamp room and watch the sea for as long as you like.

II

Three things you learn on the rock, and nothing else to carry

i

Light is the only tool you have

There is no inventory. You turn the lens, shutter the panes and trim the wick. A beam held on the right bearing lights a reef marker, a window on the mainland, or a figure on the gallery rail.

Lens puzzles

ii

The tower changes with the tide

Saltmarrow’s lower rooms flood and drain twice a day. Read the tide table in the log and you know which door is open at 04:40, and which stair is under water.

Rooms

Tidal range

iii

Every log entry is evidence

No quest markers and no glowing hints. Brand’s journal is the map. Check his dates against the weather, the oil stores and the tide, and the gaps start to say something.
