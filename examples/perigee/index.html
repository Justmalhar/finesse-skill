01

Why stars streak

The Earth turns once a day, so the whole sky wheels around the celestial pole at 15 degrees an hour. Leave the shutter open on a fixed tripod and every star draws an arc.

02

Perigee turns the other way

A motor rotates your camera around an axis parallel to the Earth’s, at the sky’s own rate. The stars hold still in the frame, so the exposure gathers faint light instead of smearing it. It carries up to 5 kg of camera and lens.

03

It finds the pole by itself

Tracking only works when that axis points at the celestial pole. Perigee’s built-in camera reads the stars around it and guides you on in about 90 seconds, then tracks for 20 hours on one charge.
